Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers about supplier verification, supported countries, pricing, and our demo platform.

Can any business list products immediately?
No. Unlike open classifieds, AfriTrade Connect requires mandatory identity and statutory business checks. Suppliers must submit verified government registration certificates (such as CAC in Nigeria, CIPC in South Africa, or RGD in Ghana). Until an administrator verifies these documents, product listings remain disabled.
Which countries are currently supported?
Our active trade network currently covers Nigeria, South Africa, and Ghana. Verified businesses located in these three nations can list lots, request quotes, and arrange cross-border deliveries.
How do currency estimates work?
Suppliers list lots in their native local currency (NGN, ZAR, GHS, or USD). As a buyer, you can toggle the currency selector to view indicative conversions in your own home currency. The conversion rate reflects transparent mid-market rates without hidden bank surcharges or unexpected broker markups.
Is real money required for this phase?
No, zero real money is required. The platform currently operates in a simulated demo mode. All supplier registrations, quotes, test payments, and tracking logs use safe test credits. This allows wholesale buyers and local businesses to experience the full end-to-end workflow risk-free.
