# AfriTrade Connect — Anti-Generic UI/UX Evaluation Rubric

> **Authority & Source Citations**: `design/stitch/ANSWERS.md` (Q7, Q8, Q9, Q14, Q20, Q21, Q26, Q31, Q32), `docs/product/01-PRD.md`.  
> **Status**: Objective, Testable Pass/Fail Criteria for Stitch Outputs.

---

## 1. Purpose & Testable Evaluation Philosophy

This rubric provides **strictly objective, testable pass/fail gates** to evaluate all screens generated by Google Stitch. Its purpose is to guarantee that the generated interfaces are custom-tailored to high-stakes intra-African B2B cross-border commerce and completely free from generic AI template clichés.

---

## 2. The 10 "Default-AI-UI Smells" Ban List (Immediate Failure Criteria)

Every Stitch screen generation must be audited against these 10 banned AI generation patterns. A single violation constitutes a **FAIL**:

| Smells / Anti-Patterns | Description of Banned Pattern | Required AfriTrade Connect Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Gradient Hero Blobs** | Amorphous blurred background gradient circles (purple, teal, neon green) floating behind text. | Clean, structured industrial backgrounds: crisp borders, solid surfaces, or agricultural emerald/harvest tones (`#064e3b`, `#047857`). |
| **2. Three-Icon Feature Cards** | Generic triad cards with vague buzzwords ("Fast", "Secure", "Scalable") and placeholder text. | Domain-specific flowcards: (1) Register as Buyer/Supplier, (2) Corporate KYB Approval, (3) Bulk Order Placement, (4) Curated Freight Tracking. |
| **3. Interchangeable SaaS Grids** | Floating card grids with identical borders and rounded corners that look like a generic fintech template. | Purpose-built B2B trade structures: wholesale commodity cards with MOQ, bulk unit weights, and dual settlement pricing. |
| **4. System-Font-Only Typography** | Falling back to generic system sans-serif without character or hierarchy. | Explicit hierarchy: **Bricolage Grotesque** (Headings) + **Noto Sans** (Body/UI) + Tabular figures for money. |
| **5. Unthemed Table Placeholders** | Gray wireframe tables with generic column headers ("Column 1", "Data A"). | Data-dense tables with authentic trade fields: Consignment ID, Buyer Corporate Name, Origin/Destination corridor, Carrier, Minor-unit Total. |
| **6. Lorem Ipsum & Dummy Latin** | Any instance of `Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet...` or generic Latin filler. | **100% authentic domain copy** ([AGENT DRAFT] reviewed by owner). Realistic agricultural and industrial trade terminology. |
| **7. Generic Stock-Photo Mood** | Suits in glass corporate skyscrapers, generic smiling handshakes, or irrelevant western cityscapes. | Authentic African industrial and agricultural imagery: grain warehousing, cocoa sacks, cargo shipping containers, port terminals. |
| **8. Identical Public & Admin Layouts** | Giving the administrative operations portal the same open, marketing-heavy layout as the landing page. | Clear separation: `apps/web` is mobile-first and conversion-driven; `apps/admin` is a data-dense, left-sidebar **operational cockpit**. |
| **9. Centered-Everything Hero** | Hero section with centered text, centered subtext, two identical centered buttons, and empty margins. | Asymmetrical, high-converting hero layout with clear primary/secondary CTAs, live trade corridors, and active currency tickers (`ANSWERS.md` Q26). |
| **10. Emoji as Icons** | Using consumer emojis (🚀, 📦, 🌍, 💰) in place of structured interface iconography. | **Phosphor Icons (Duotone weight)** configured across all navigation, buttons, and state chips (`ANSWERS.md` Q21). |

## 3. Specificity Checklist: Intra-African B2B Trade Domain Markers

A generated screen must actively demonstrate that it belongs to **AfriTrade Connect** by visibly incorporating authentic trade domain markers:

### 3.1 Trade Corridors & Geography
- Origin and destination indicators representing **Nigeria (NG)**, **South Africa (ZA)**, and **Ghana (GH)** with authentic regional ports (e.g. Apapa Port Lagos, Durban Container Terminal, Port of Tema Accra).
- AfCFTA (African Continental Free Trade Area) preferential trade context clearly stated.

### 3.2 Real B2B Currencies & Dual Pricing
- Visible multi-currency presence: `NGN (₦)`, `ZAR (R)`, `GHS (GH₵)`, and `USD ($)`.
- Explicit dual-price formatting on all listings:  
  `GHS 150.00 / 50kg bag`  
  `≈ NGN 15,200 (indicative)` with tap explanation.

### 3.3 Wholesale Commercial Lot Units
- Units are **wholesale and commercial**, never consumer single-item retail:
  - Metric Tonnes (MT)
  - 50kg Jute Bags
  - Pallets / Crates
  - 20ft / 40ft TEU Shipping Containers
  - Minimum Order Quantities (e.g. `MOQ: 10 Metric Tonnes`)

### 3.4 Authentic Agricultural & Industrial Commodities
- Realistic trade catalog items:
  - Yellow Maize Grade 1 (Animal Feed)
  - Fermented Dried Cocoa Beans (Export Grade)
  - Raw Cashew Nuts (In-Shell)
  - Industrial Cassava Starch (99% Purity)
  - White Refined Sesame Seeds
  - Palm Kernel Cake / Oil

### 3.5 Real Operational & Logistics Stages
- Order states reflect physical cargo movements:
  `Order placed` ➔ `Payment confirmed` ➔ `Logistics assigned` ➔ `Picked up` ➔ `In transit` ➔ `At customs` ➔ `Delivered`.
- Mentions of curated logistics partners covering specific West and Southern African transit routes.

### 3.6 Regulatory & Compliance Markers
- Corporate KYB references: CAC (Corporate Affairs Commission, Nigeria), CIPC (Companies and Intellectual Property Commission, South Africa), GRA (Ghana Revenue Authority).
- Audit decision logs with mandatory reviewer rationale.

## 5. Scoring Matrix & Review Verdict

For every screen reviewed from Stitch:

- **Pass**: Zero AI smells detected; satisfies all 6 domain specificity markers; meets owner criteria Q7–Q9; fully accessible in light and dark mode.
- **Fail / Revise**: Any AI smell detected, missing dual-price copy, consumer retail terminology found, or generic placeholder copy present. Must be edited via `edit_screens` with explicit corrective prompt before code handoff.
